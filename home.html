Barichara, Santander

Why should your chosen destination definitely be on your bucket list?
Have you ever dreamed of a place where time stops? Barichara is a mandatory destination for travelers in Colombia. This town is famous for its yellow stone architecture and its very peaceful atmosphere. It is the perfect spot if you want to relax and enjoy history.

Start Exploring

Where to Go

The Cathedral

You must visit the Cathedral of the Immaculate Conception because its architecture is unique in Santander.

Santa Barbara Chapel

You should go up to Santa Barbara Chapel at the top of the hill. When you look around from the viewpoints, you can see the amazing Suarez Canyon.

What to Do

Hike the Camino Real

If you walk the Camino Real to Guane, you will find out many things about colonial history and enjoy nature.

Salto del Mico

You should hike up to Salto del Mico to take beautiful photos of the mountains.

Stone Carving Workshop

If you visit a local workshop, you can learn how to carve stone like a professional.

Taste Local Food

If you eat "hormigas culonas", you experience a very traditional and unique flavor of Santander.

Travel Tips

  • 1. Comfortable Shoes: You have to wear comfortable shoes because the streets are made of stone.
  • 2. Sun Protection: You must put on sunscreen every day to protect your skin from the strong sun.
  • 3. Money: You should carry cash because some small shops and artisans do not accept credit cards.
  • 4. Culture: You must not forget to be respectful with the local people and their quiet traditions.
